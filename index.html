Available for new projects

Most CRM teams ask how to keep users subscribed. I start with what your product actually means to them, and whether they feel understood along the way.

That question comes from a background in psychology and communications, and a long fascination with why people form habits and build relationships with each other and their devices (often for reasons they couldn't name in a survey). Over the past decade, I've turned that understanding into CRM, leading and scaling lifecycle strategies for products like Blinkist, TIER Mobility and Slowly across 22+ markets.

The landscape of CRM is changing fast, and your users' lock screens don't need more AI slop. I help your brand stand out with a human-first, AI-backed strategy, where every interaction carries intention and emotion, and converts into direct results.

What I do

Your users stay where they feel understood.

I work on CRM for B2C apps and subscription products, where retention quietly decides everything else. Whether your CRM fits under Product, Tech or Marketing, I'll be at home in all three. I bring experience across creative execution, technical setup and leadership, ensuring strategy and delivery stay closely connected in whatever way your team needs.

  1. Strategy

    • Retention roadmaps that set priorities for every lifecycle stage, from trial to win-back.
    • Behavioural segments and personas your whole programme works from.
    • Platform selection and migration, plus the team processes that keep it all running.
    • AI workflows designed around how your team actually works, so a small team can ship more without cutting corners.

  2. Execution

    • Automated journeys, from the welcome series to renewal reminders.
    • Campaigns across email, push, in-app and content cards, written in your brand voice.
    • Modular templates and Liquid personalisation, QA'd before every send.
    • AI-assisted copy and production with clear guardrails, so output scales and your brand voice stays intact.

  3. Optimisation

    • A/B tests on message, timing and channel mix.
    • Offers and push cadence tuned to convert, without fatiguing users or training them to wait for a discount.
    • Deliverability, and KPIs that tie CRM to revenue and renewals, not just opens and clicks.
    • Faster test cycles, using AI to draft variants and pull results together, so learnings turn into action sooner.
