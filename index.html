Available for new projects

Most CRM teams ask how to keep users subscribed. I start with what your product actually means to them, and whether they feel understood along the way.

With a background in psychology and communications, I love learning why people form habits and build relationships with each other and their devices (often for reasons they couldn't name in a survey). Over the past decade, I've turned that understanding into CRM, leading and scaling lifecycle strategies for products like Blinkist, TIER Mobility and Slowly across 22+ markets.

The landscape of CRM is changing fast, and I know your users' lock screens don't need more AI slop. I help your brand stand out with a human-first, psychology & AI-backed strategy, where every interaction carries intention and emotion, and converts into direct results.

CRM for B2C apps and subscription products across strategy and delivery.

  1. Strategy

    Retention roadmaps for every stage, from trial to win-back. Behavioural segments and personas your whole programme can build on. Platform selection and migration, without disrupting live sends. And AI workflows that fit the way your team already works.

  2. Execution

    Automated journeys, from the welcome series to renewal reminders. Campaigns across email, push and in-app, written in your brand voice. Modular templates and personalisation, checked before every send. And AI-assisted production that scales without losing your voice.

  3. Optimisation

    A/B tests on message, timing and channel. Offers and cadence that convert, without wearing users out. Deliverability, plus KPIs that tie CRM to revenue. And faster testing cycles, with AI handling the heavy lifting.
